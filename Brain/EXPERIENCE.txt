MARCEL BRAIN — NEURAL REDESIGN / LOCAL OPTICAL REVIEW

The main view now uses an animated neural light network: flowing shader pulses,
cyan/blue/violet filaments, amber highlights, pulsing nodes, drifting particles,
restrained bloom and a holographic pedestal. No opaque anatomical/STL sculpture
is displayed. Existing licensed surface geometry is only a shape guide for the
organic light paths and a faint holographic rim; its asset license remains valid.
The effects are artistic, not neural telemetry. Actual chat requests increase
visual intensity through existing busy/error state, without model/API changes.

The globe is a separate Three.js view. Moscow, Nairobi, Berlin and Medellin have
static approximate city-center coordinates. City buttons smoothly rotate and
zoom to their target; markers highlight the selection. Globe markers are also
clickable. Drag, keyboard, wheel and explicit zoom/reset controls are available.
The city panel truthfully states that news and weather are not connected.

Future integration boundary:
- Listen for document event brain-globe-focus (id/name/country/lat/lon).
- An authorized server adapter may fetch licensed, sourced regional information.
- Supply display data through MarcelBrainGlobe.setContents(cityId, items), where
  each item contains title/source/https URL. Stale city responses and unsafe URLs
  are rejected; text is not treated as HTML. No adapter/provider is enabled now.
- No city choice becomes a personal fact, relocation plan or live-state update.

Preserved: existing chat APIs, conversations, sources, TXT export, learning and
manual imports, personal/doc knowledge, filters, live state, crown review and
platform navigation. The companion memory/recent-conversation panel reuses
existing rendered state; no additional private-knowledge/model requests.
No production DB migration, paid model call, new framework or auth change.

Open source and map data:
Three.js r180 / 0.180.0, MIT; local EffectComposer, UnrealBloomPass and supporting
passes/shaders. Their imports were adjusted only to the pinned local module.
See vendor/manifest.json and THREE-LICENSE.txt.
Neural shape guide: Nevit Dilmen, CC BY-SA 3.0; see assets/ATTRIBUTION.txt.
Globe coastlines: Natural Earth 1:110m land, Public Domain. See earth-source.json
and https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . All map assets are
served locally. Texture speckles are decorative, not live or population data.

Performance: capped frame rate/pixel ratio, smaller mobile guide, stopped loops
for hidden/offscreen views, reduced-motion support and independent SVG fallbacks.
Mobile places the globe below the input and measures composer height so expanded
options/errors do not overlap it. Genuine iPhone/Safari hardware QA remains open.

Preview: node test/brain-experience.preview.mjs from repository root.
Open http://127.0.0.1:4591/Brain/ . Preview owner APIs are synthetic and in-memory;
never expose this fixture server publicly. Focused browser functions are in
brain-experience.browser.js and brain-neural.browser.js under test/.
Reference images guide appearance only; fake news, prices, subscriptions,
"millions of sources", neural telemetry and unsupported tools were not copied.
